VESAGE — MASTER CREATIVE WEBSITE + CLAUDE CODE PROMPT
You are building the official portfolio website for VESAGE, an independent creative practice working across advertising, film, brand content, creative direction, and digital/web experiences.

This is NOT a conventional marketing-agency website.

The website itself must feel like an artist's work.

Think:

Independent artist × film director × luxury editorial × creative studio × digital art exhibition

The website must communicate two things simultaneously:

1. VESAGE has strong artistic taste and a distinctive visual identity.
2. VESAGE is commercially capable of creating serious advertising, campaigns, films, brand content, and websites for real brands.

The final result should feel premium, cinematic, minimal, bold, artistic, and highly intentional.

---

01 — BRAND IDENTITY

Brand name:

VESAGE

Positioning:

«An independent creative practice making films, campaigns, brands and digital experiences with an artist's eye.»

Do NOT make the website feel like:

- a generic digital agency
- a social media agency
- a web-development company
- a SaaS website
- a corporate marketing company
- a template portfolio
- a standard Behance-style grid
- an over-designed "agency website" full of unnecessary animations

The visitor should feel like they have entered the visual world of a creative artist/studio.

The desired emotional reaction is:

"Who made this?"

followed by:

"I want to see their work."

and finally:

"These people can make something serious for my brand."

«Quiet luxury with cinematic impact.»

quietly confident

46 — DEVELOPMENT PROCESS
Do not blindly build everything at once.
Work in this order:
PHASE 1 — INSPECT
Inspect the existing codebase.
Determine:
framework
styling system
routing
existing dependencies
animation libraries
asset system
video handling
responsive structure
Do not replace the stack unless necessary.
PHASE 2 — FOUNDATION
Build:
typography
colors
spacing
navigation
project data architecture
responsive foundation
PHASE 3 — HERO
Make the hero exceptional.
Do not move forward until it feels like VESAGE.
PHASE 4 — PORTFOLIO
Build the 10-project exhibition.
Prioritize the real uploaded assets.
PHASE 5 — PROJECT PAGES
Build reusable project templates.
PHASE 6 — LAB
Introduce experimental/Higgsfield content.
PHASE 7 — ABOUT + CONTACT
Keep them minimal.
PHASE 8 — MOTION
Only after the static composition is strong.
PHASE 9 — MOBILE
Design mobile intentionally.
PHASE 10 — PERFORMANCE
Optimize all videos and images.
PHASE 11 — FINAL ART DIRECTION PASS
Review the entire website as a creative director.
Ask:
Does it look expensive?
Does it look artistic?
Does it feel cinematic?
Does it feel like VESAGE?
Does it look different from a normal agency website?
Are the projects the hero?
Are we using too many effects?
Can anything be removed?
Does the website itself demonstrate the quality of VESAGE?
47 — MOST IMPORTANT FINAL INSTRUCTION
Do not optimize for "more."
Optimize for:
TASTE.
The website should make a potential client believe:
If VESAGE can make their own website look this good, imagine what they can make for our brand.
That is the ultimate purpose of this portfolio.
Build VESAGE as an artwork first, portfolio second, and business website third.
